Review severity: what blocks, what becomes debt

Grade each review finding by how likely it is against what it breaks. Only Blocker and Major findings block a merge and cost a fix round. Minor findings go to a tech-debt list per repository, and Nits are just counted. On our last four runs (236 findings), blocking findings drop from 143 to 109, and 8 fix and re-review rounds (796 credits, 75 minutes) would not have run.

Data: runs of 2026-10-27 to -30, graded by hand by one grader (not yet cross-checked). Per-finding grades are in research/2026-10-30-review-severity/findings.csv; round costs come from orchestrator-workers mine.py.

A Blocker or Major must carry a failure scenario, file:line and VERIFIED or INFERRED. An INFERRED Major is reproduced before it is fixed. Pre-existing problems and report Corrections are separate and never cost a fix round.

The review system, end to end

1. Tools firsttypes, lint, tests, axe, build; add a secret scan and start-up budget
→
2. Review by riskrisk areas get max effort and their checklist; others the base list
→
3. Grade every findinglikelihood × impact, with a scenario and file:line for Majors
→
4. RouteBlocker or Major: fix round. Minor: debt list. Nit: count. Correction: fix the record
→
5. Rule and recordorchestrator accepts, demotes or rejects; owner can overrule; one line each
→
6. Calibrateacceptance rate per area; a second grader on 40 rows, aim kappa ≥ 0.6
  • Re-reviews check only the fixed findings and the fix diff (8 fixes caused new defects). One extra round when one is left open, then the owner decides.
  • Debt is paid when a later item touches the same file (fixes under about 15 minutes), when a class repeats 3 times, or when the list passes about 30 rows.
  • The main risk: a real problem graded "contrived". It is watched by the second grader and by reopening any demoted row a real defect hits.
