Router: effort, context and enforcement

Four unattended runs, 2026-10-27 to 10-30: 202 worker rounds, 201 ended. Companion to research/2026-10-30-router-effort-context.md. Costs and times only: harder items went to Opus and briefs differ, so these bars do not compare quality.

At one per-token price, effort decides the bill

Sonnet at high cost 2.3× Sonnet at medium on fix rounds, and Opus high build rounds had the widest spread (P90 more than twice the median).

Minutes follow output tokens

In an unattended window, minutes are the budget that binds. Medium halved the median wall time of Sonnet fix and build rounds.

Higher effort spends most of its output on reasoning

Reasoning tokens as a share of output tokens, per cell. At medium, Sonnet's reasoning share fell to about a quarter.

Claude rounds compact silently

A drop in context from at least 75% of the window to below 60% of its prior value, between tool calls: 49 such drops in 36 rounds, at 80–88% of the window. No log event records them.

Where the credits went

Output dominates, so effort is the main cost lever; cache reads are the cost of carrying a long context on every call.
