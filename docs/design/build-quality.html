Build quality: why first reviews block

Four unattended runs, 2026-10-27 to 10-30: 37 first reviews, 264 findings, 133 accepted blockers. Companion to research/2026-10-30-build-quality.md; data in research/2026-10-30-build-quality/findings.csv.

Most blockers are cases nobody listed, not briefs that said nothing

Missed event orders, adjacent paths and unchecked library facts make half of the accepted blockers; with wrong structures, edge values and failure paths, three quarters. A brief silent on the rule caused 2.

A quarter arose after the first build

Fix rounds fixed the case a finding named and left a sibling of the same class, or broke something nearby. The fix template says "do not widen the scope".

Where it stands today

Target: 25% clean and at most 1.5 blockers per first review over the next 25 first reviews. Decide on blockers per review: a drop to 1.5 has about 75 to 80% power after 12 first reviews; the clean rate needs about 59 per group.
