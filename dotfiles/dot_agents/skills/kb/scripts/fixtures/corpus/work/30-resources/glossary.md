# Glossary

Terms used across Larkspur Freight. One row per meaning; a term with two meanings gets two rows.

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **3PL** | Third-party logistics provider: a partner that runs warehousing or transport for a shipper. |
| **ASN** | Advance ship notice: the message telling a dock what is coming, sent before the truck arrives. |
| **BOL** | Bill of lading: the shipping document listing goods, shipper, consignee and carrier; the carrier's receipt for the freight. |
| **DLQ** | Dead-letter queue: where a message goes after it fails processing too many times. |
| **DWELL** | Dwell time: minutes a truck spends on site, from gate-in to gate-out. |
| **EDI** | Electronic data interchange: structured messages (tenders, invoices, status updates) exchanged with carriers and customers. |
| **ETA** | Estimated time of arrival at a stop or dock. |
| **ETD** | Estimated time of departure from a stop or dock. |
| **FTL** | Full truckload: one shipment fills the trailer. |
| **LTL** | Less than truckload: several shippers share one trailer; priced by weight and freight class. |
| **OTIF** | On time in full: share of orders delivered inside the window and complete. |
| **POD** | Proof of delivery: signed or photographed confirmation that a shipment was received. |
| **POD (k8s)** | Kubernetes pod: the smallest deployable unit on the platform, one or more containers. Not proof of delivery. |
| **RMA** | Return merchandise authorization: approval for a customer to send goods back. |
| **SLA** | Service level agreement: what we promise a customer, with credits if we miss it. |
| **SLO** | Service level objective: an internal reliability target, stricter than the SLA. |
| **TMS** | Transportation management system: plans and tenders loads; route-planner feeds it. |
| **WMS** | Warehouse management system: inventory and picking inside the building. |
| **YMS** | Yard management system: tracks trailers in the yard and their moves to doors. |
| **Door** | A dock door; each door has bookable slots in dock-scheduler. |
| **Slot** | A time window on a door that a carrier can hold for loading or unloading. |
