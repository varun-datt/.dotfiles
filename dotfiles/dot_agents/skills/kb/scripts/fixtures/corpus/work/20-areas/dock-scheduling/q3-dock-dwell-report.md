# Q3 dock dwell report

Period: July to September 2026. Dwell = gate-in to gate-out, in minutes (see dwell-time-definitions).

| Site | Median dwell | p90 dwell | Change in median vs Q2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| North | 48 | 131 | -9 |
| South | 55 | 162 | +4 |
| East cross-dock | 31 | 74 | -2 |

Findings
- North improved after the early-arrival hold went live in August.
- South's p90 is driven by live unloads of floor-loaded LTL, on average 38 minutes longer than palletized freight.
- 6% of visits have no gate-out scan; they are excluded and listed in the appendix.

Recommendations
- Extend the early-arrival hold to South in Q4.
- Require a gate-out scan before a door is marked free.
