# Slot overbooking study

Question: about 9% of booked dock slots go unused (no-show or very late). Could we book 105-110% of door capacity without raising dwell?

Method
- Replay Q2 and Q3 bookings through a simulator with overbooking ratios from 100% to 115%.
- Measure median and p90 dwell, detention minutes, and how often a truck finds no door.

Status: simulator done; first results in findings-2026-09.
Owner: dock-scheduling team. Deadline: mid November.
