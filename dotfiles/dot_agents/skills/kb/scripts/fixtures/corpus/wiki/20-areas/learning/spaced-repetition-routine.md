# Spaced repetition routine

Review a card just before you would forget it. Each successful review pushes the next one further out (1 day, 3 days, 8 days, 3 weeks...); a failure resets the interval.

My rules
- 15 minutes each morning, before email.
- Ten new cards a day at most; the review pile grows faster than you expect.
- One fact per card. If the answer needs a paragraph, split it.
- Write cards in my own words, never paste.
- Suspend leeches (cards failed 6+ times) and rewrite them.

The interval schedule looks like exponential backoff: each success spaces the next attempt further out, and a failure starts over.
