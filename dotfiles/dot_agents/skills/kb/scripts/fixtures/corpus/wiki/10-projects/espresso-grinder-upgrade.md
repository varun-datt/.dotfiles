# Espresso grinder upgrade

Why: the current grinder drifts coarser over a week and shots run fast (18 g in, 36 g out in 19 s instead of 27-30 s).

Criteria
- Stepless adjustment.
- Retention under 0.5 g.
- Fits under the cabinet (max 40 cm tall).

Shortlist: three flat-burr models in the mid price range; decide after reading up on burr alignment.
