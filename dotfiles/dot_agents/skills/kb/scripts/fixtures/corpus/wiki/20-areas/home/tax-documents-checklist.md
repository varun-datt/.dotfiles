# Tax documents checklist

Where each document lives. No figures in this note on purpose.

- [ ] Employer wage statement: arrives by email in late January; saved to the encrypted drive, folder `Taxes/<year>/income`.
- [ ] Bank interest statements: download from each bank's site; same folder.
- [ ] Brokerage year-end summary: download in mid February; folder `Taxes/<year>/investments`.
- [ ] Charitable receipts: paper copies in the blue binder, section C; scan into `Taxes/<year>/deductions`.
- [ ] Home office receipts: photographed into `Taxes/<year>/deductions/office`.
- [ ] Last year's return: `Taxes/<previous year>/filed`.

The encrypted drive's recovery key is kept in the safe, never in a note.
