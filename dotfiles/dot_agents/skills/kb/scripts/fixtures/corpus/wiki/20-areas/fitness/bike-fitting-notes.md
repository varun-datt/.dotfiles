# Bike fitting notes

Fit session in March; what changed:
- Saddle height (center of bottom bracket to top of saddle): lowered 4 mm.
- Saddle setback: unchanged.
- Stem: 10 mm shorter; reach felt stretched on long climbs.
- Cleats: moved 2 mm back; numb toes after 60 km went away.

Symptoms to watch
- Pain at the front of the knee: saddle too low or too far forward.
- Hips rocking on the saddle: saddle too high.

Next: re-check after 1,000 km; the fitter offered a free follow-up within three months.
