# Tagging conventions

- Lowercase and hyphenated: `bike-fit`, not `BikeFit`.
- One tag for the area (`home`, `fitness`, `learning`) plus up to three subject tags.
- `inbox` only while a note sits in `00-inbox`.
- Add `archive` when a note moves to `40-archive`; keep its old tags.
- No date tags; the `created` field is enough.
